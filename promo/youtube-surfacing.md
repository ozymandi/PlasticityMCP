## Description

PlasticityMCP now does free-form surfacing. An LLM drives Plasticity through 145 MCP tools and builds real CAD surfaces — no mouse: a 1955 Maserati 300S from one side drawing, and a 6 m boat, CUBE, from a single photo.

What's new in this update:
• patch — fills an opening with G0 / G1 / G2 continuity to its neighbours
• loft_edges — joins two surfaces along their edges, clamped at both ends
• sweep_profile — a section swept along 1–3 rails
• fillet — round, conic and G2 blends
• measure_continuity — checks the angle between face normals across a seam (G2 at 0.000002°)

The drawing sheets come from export_drawing: 7 views per model, visible and hidden lines, as SVG.

Every surface in the video is native Plasticity B-Rep made by the model. The zebra and the mesh are rendered from the same geometry, the shots are Cycles renders in Blender.
